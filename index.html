The Greeks, in plain English

Δ

Delta

How much an option's price moves when the underlying moves $1. The closest thing options have to "directional exposure."

Θ

Theta

Time decay — what a premium seller is paid to wait. The engine behind most of TheGreeksDesk's content.

Γ

Gamma

How fast Delta changes. The reason short-dated options can turn against a position quickly near expiry.

Ρ

Rho

Sensitivity to interest rates — smaller day-to-day, but part of why longer-dated pricing shifts with rate expectations.
