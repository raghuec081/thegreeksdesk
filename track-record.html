First month trading SPXW/VIXW 0DTE iron-condor-style spreads alongside NDXP and the IREN wheel. High win rate, but 0DTE index spreads that finish ITM settle in cash at the full width of the spread rather than a typical stop-loss amount. The 31 Mar call spread is a clean example: a $43.74 credit turned into a $956.26 loss because both legs settled deep in the money. This is the structural risk of selling short-dated index spreads: small, frequent wins against occasional losses that can run to the full defined-risk amount. The 100 IREN shares from a February put assignment were carried as a covered-call position through March and sold in April for a small loss on the stock itself, separate from the option premium collected along the way.
