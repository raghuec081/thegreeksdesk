About

TheGreeksDesk is written by one person, not a team, someone who spends the day job managing risk data for a major bank, and the evenings trading NDX and SPX option spreads, mostly short dated.

This isn't a signals service. There's nothing here to "copy." No bot, no black-box algorithm, no premium Discord promising winning trades. It's plain English education on how options actually work, and just as importantly, how to manage the risk properly, written by someone whose day job is literally risk management, just applied to a bank's balance sheet instead of a personal account.

Why stay anonymous? Because the content should stand on its own. If an explanation of time decay or position sizing only makes sense because of who's saying it, it wasn't a good explanation. Judge what's written here, not who wrote it.

Everything published is educational only. Nothing on this site is personalised financial advice, and nothing should be read as a recommendation to buy or sell anything. See the track record page for an honest, public account of what's actually being traded wins and losses both.
